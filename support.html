Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my water intake?

Open HydroLog and use the water logging controls to record your drinks. Your daily progress updates automatically.

Can I change my daily water goal?

Yes. You can adjust your personal daily water goal in the app.

How do I enable reminders?

Open Settings and configure your water reminders. Make sure notifications are allowed for HydroLog in your device settings.

Do I need to create an account?

No. HydroLog does not require registration or sign-in.

Is HydroLog free?

Yes. HydroLog is free to use. Optional rewarded advertisements are available in the Support HydroLog section.

Is HydroLog a medical app?

No. HydroLog is intended for general wellness and habit tracking. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
